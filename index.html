About Us

Lights Out for Youth is a youth-led organization dedicated to protecting our disappearing night sky. We connect students who care about light pollution and give them ways to take action in their own communities through education, local pledges, and advocacy.

Outreach

LOFY members give presentations introducing students to light pollution and showing them how they can help protect dark skies.

Pledges

LOFY pledges turn awareness into action by bringing students together to make simple changes that protect the night sky and the environment.
