Before Lights Out for Youth was an organization, a coalition of students, or even an idea, it started with a 10-year-old watching a meteor shower.

In her Idaho backyard, Renata spent her summer nights stargazing. Exceptionally dark skies were a norm in her small town, and seeing the vastness of the universe overhead sparked a fascination with astronomy that only grew as she got older.
